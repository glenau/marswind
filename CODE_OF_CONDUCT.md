# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people.

## What that means here

Expected:

- Assume the person on the other side is trying to help.
- Criticize the change, not whoever wrote it.
- Take "no" without escalating, and give "no" with a reason.
- Remember that English is a second language for many contributors, including
  the maintainer. Read for intent.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Comments about someone's identity - nationality, gender, sexuality, religion,
  disability, age, appearance, or anything else in that family.
- Sexual language or imagery, anywhere in the project.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, brigading a thread, or
  demanding unpaid work from a maintainer.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and
anywhere else the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Email **glenaudev@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer only and
are not shared further without your agreement.

Include what happened, where, and links if you have them. You will get a reply
within a week.

## What happens then

Depending on what happened and whether it is a pattern: a private word, a public
correction, a request to edit or withdraw a comment, or a block from the
repository. Severe or repeated behaviour goes straight to a block. The
maintainer decides, and will say what was decided and why to the person who
reported it.

## Scope

This project's spaces, and public spaces where someone is representing the
project.
